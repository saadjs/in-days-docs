Getting started

What is InDays?

InDays helps hybrid workers keep track of the days they go into the office. Log days, plan the ones ahead, review your history, set targets, and get gentle reminders, all in one simple app.

Which devices does InDays work on?

InDays runs on iPhone and iPad. The companion Apple Watch app works with a Watch paired to your iPhone, and widgets are available on the Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Do I need to create an account?

No. InDays works without an account, and your data stays on your device unless you choose to sync it.

If you want your data on more than one device, you can sign in with Apple, Google, or GitHub at any time.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Logging, planning, history, reminders, widgets, and the Watch app all work offline. You only need a connection to sign in, sync, or load the latest prebuilt filters.

Logging & planning

How do I log an office day?

Open the Log tab. Today is selected by default, so just tap Log today. To record a different office for that entry, tap Change next to the office location first.

You can also log from a Home Screen widget, the Apple Watch app, Siri, or a Shortcuts automation. These use your primary office if you've set one.

Can I log multiple days at once?

Yes. On the Log tab, tap Add day (or Add more) next to Selected days and choose up to 14 days, then log them together. It's handy for catching up on days you forgot. Days you've already logged are skipped.

Can I edit or delete a day I already logged?

Yes. Open History → Manage logged days, pick the entry, and change its date or office, or delete it.

Can I plan days I haven't gone in yet?

Yes. On the Log tab, tap today or any future date on the calendar to plan it. To plan against a target, tap a saved filter's progress card, or Plan days in Saved filters, to open its planner. You can plan up to a year ahead.

Planning a day doesn't log it. A planned day shows as upcoming, becomes kept once you log that date, and shows as missed if the day passes without a log.

How do I remove a planned day?

Tap the planned day again on the calendar to unplan it. Unplanning never deletes a logged entry.

Can I save more than one office?

Yes. Go to Profile → Locations to save up to five offices, and mark one as your primary office.

What is the primary office used for?

Your primary office is attached automatically when you log, including from widgets, the Apple Watch, Siri, and Shortcuts. In the app you can pick a different office for any entry. If you haven't set a primary office, logs are saved without a location.

Reminders, widgets & automation

What are saved and prebuilt filters?

Saved filters are reusable date ranges, each with an optional target number of office days. Create your own, or start from a prebuilt range and adjust it. InDays shows your progress and tells you when you've hit the target.

You can keep up to 12 saved filters, pin up to three for widgets, and open any of them in the planner to place the days you still need.

Can I set up reminders?

Yes. Go to Profile → Reminders, turn on the end-of-day prompt, and choose the time and the weekdays you want it.

Each reminder has a Log today action, so you can record the day straight from the notification. Reminders are scheduled on your device and need notification permission the first time you turn them on.

Do planned days get their own reminders?

Yes. With reminders on, InDays nudges you the evening before a day you planned, then asks again that evening if the day still isn't logged. The evening-before nudge has no log button, because you can't log a day before it happens.

Are there Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets?

Yes. Pin up to three saved filters from Saved filters and they appear in the widgets. The first pinned filter is the one shown on the Lock Screen. Home Screen widgets show your progress and can log today without opening the app.

Does InDays have an Apple Watch app?

Yes. The Watch app shows whether you've logged today, your primary office, and how many days you've been in this week. Tap Log today to record the day from your wrist.

It stays in sync with your iPhone automatically. There's nothing to set up beyond installing it.

Can I use Siri?

Yes. Say “Log in office day in InDays” or run the Log In-Office shortcut. Your primary office is used automatically if you've set one.

Can I automate logging with Shortcuts?

Yes. In the Shortcuts app, create a Personal Automation and add InDays' Log In-Office Day action. Run it when you arrive at the office, when you leave, or at whatever time suits your routine.

InDays doesn't ask for location permission. Arrival and departure triggers are handled entirely by Apple's Shortcuts app.

A Shortcuts automation can run on arrival, departure, a time of day, and many other triggers.

Where can I see what's new?

InDays shows a What's New sheet the first time you open the app after an update with something worth calling out. You can open it again anytime from Profile → What's New.

Account, sync & data

Which sign-in methods are available?

Apple, Google, and GitHub. Signing in is optional; skip it and InDays keeps everything on your device.

How does syncing work?

When you're signed in, your logs, planned days, office locations, and saved filters sync so they appear on your other devices. Changes sync automatically whenever you're online, and you can pull to refresh to sync right away.

When you sign out, InDays clears synced account data from the device once syncing finishes. If some changes haven't synced yet, it keeps them on the device so they can upload the next time you sign in.

What if a sync fails?

Your changes stay saved on the device. Profile shows a Cloud sync failed notice; once you're back online, tap Retry. Wait for a successful sync before expecting the changes on another device.

What data does the app collect?

Only what you enter and what the app needs to run: your logs, planned days, office locations, profile details, saved filters, reminder settings, and basic account details if you sign in. There are no ads, analytics, or tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data or account?

You can delete individual entries from History → Manage logged days. To delete your account and all synced data, open Profile → Account and tap Delete account. This removes your cloud data, clears local data on the device, and signs you out.

If you signed in with Apple, you'll be asked to confirm with Apple once more so InDays can revoke its sign-in token. If that step fails, your account isn't deleted yet, so try again.

Using the app without an account? Clear everything from History → Quick actions → Data management → Wipe All Data.
