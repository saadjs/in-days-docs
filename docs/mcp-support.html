MCP Support

Email in-days@saad.sh. For the native app, see app support.

Connect

Follow the ChatGPT setup or Claude setup, then sign in with your InDays app account. The server URL is https://indays.saad.sh/mcp.

ChatGPT icon upload

The custom MCP form accepts PNG files no larger than 10 KB. Use the MCP icon, or leave the optional icon empty.

Use on iOS

Set up and install the ChatGPT plugin on the web first. Open ChatGPT on iOS with the same account and workspace, start a new chat, and select In Days using @. Availability depends on your account and workspace permissions.

App records

The MCP service uses your InDays app account. Sign in with the same Apple, Google, or GitHub account as the app. Days you log in either place show up in both once the app syncs, so sign in to the app if you have only used it signed out.

Delete data

Ask your AI app to clear specific days or delete a saved filter. To delete your account and everything in it, follow the app deletion instructions.

Disconnect

Remove InDays from your AI app's plugins or connectors. Your data stays in your InDays account.
