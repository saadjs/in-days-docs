MCP Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy covers the InDays MCP service operated by Saad Bash at https://indays.saad.sh/mcp. The native app has a separate privacy policy.

What we store

The MCP service uses your InDays app account and the same database as the app's sync. It reads and changes:

  • Logged and planned days, including dates and any locations or notes you enter.
  • Your saved filters, date ranges, and targets, and your office locations.
  • Your timezone, which the service saves to your profile.
  • Authorization records and tokens for the AI apps you connect.

Days logged through the MCP service are marked as coming from it and sync to the app like any other change.

Signing in

You sign in with the Apple, Google, or GitHub account you use in the app. We never see your provider password.

How we use your data

We use it to run the service and answer requests from the AI apps you authorize. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Service providers and AI apps

  • Supabase handles sign-in, AI app authorizations, and stores your attendance and profile data, as it does for the app.
  • Cloudflare hosts the MCP service and processes request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver it.
  • Apple, Google, or GitHub handles your chosen sign-in.
  • Your connected AI app sends requests and receives their results. Its own privacy policy governs its processing of your conversation and those results.
  • GitHub Pages hosts these documentation pages and processes requests to serve them.

The MCP service does not connect to your employer's attendance systems.

Retention and deletion

Your records remain until you delete them. Ask your AI app to clear specific days or delete saved filters. To delete your account and all its data, use Delete Account in the InDays app, as described in the app privacy policy.

Disconnecting a plugin alone does not delete your data. Deleting your account does not delete copies already held by an AI app. Service-provider backups may retain deleted data until their retention periods end.

You can also request deletion by emailing in-days@saad.sh.

Children

The service is not intended for children under 13.

Changes and contact

Changes will be posted here with an updated date. Questions? Email in-days@saad.sh.
